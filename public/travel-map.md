## What Pack does

Pack can build a visited countries and states map from organized travel records and turn travel milestones into trophies for continents, landmarks, and themed collections. The map fills in automatically from trips Pack has reconstructed, so no manual logging is needed.

## Traveler problem

Scratch-off maps, checklists, and manual travel trackers depend on the traveler to keep them current. The been-there record drifts out of date, and years of real travel end up invisible.

## How Pack helps

- Fills in countries, US states, and cities from trips already in the traveler's email and travel records.
- Keeps the map current as new confirmations and trips arrive.
- Awards travel trophies for continents, landmarks, and themed collections, each backed by a real trip.

## Inputs Pack can work from

## FAQ

### Can Pack show a map of visited countries?

Yes. Pack is designed to build a visited-countries map from organized travel records, so the map reflects trips the traveler actually took.

### Is this like a scratch-off world map?

It is the same idea without the upkeep. A scratch-off map only knows what gets scratched. Pack fills the map in from travel already in the traveler's email and trip records.

### Does the map cover US states as well as countries?

Yes. The been-there record is designed to cover states and countries visited, plus the cities and places behind them.

### What are travel trophies?

Trophies turn travel milestones into collectibles: continents completed, world landmarks reached, and themed collections. They come from the same travel record as the map, so every trophy is backed by a real trip.
